# ARGUS Protocol V5.1.0

**Release date:** 2026-09-02

ARGUS V5.1.0 is the current stable normative protocol.

This is a minor release. It adds one new analytical test â€” **modal constancy control** â€” without changing the existing categories, thresholds, proof standard, Step 7.a method, Step 7.c scale, or overall procedure of V5.0.0.

## What changes in V5.1.0

- **Step 2 â€” modal map:** load-bearing propositions are annotated by polarity and degree of commitment at each occurrence.
- **Step 6 â€” modal constancy control:** a directional test detects **directional modal retraction**, where the same load-bearing proposition is asserted strongly when it performs argumentative work and weakened when strong commitment would carry a cost.
- The guard excludes three non-defects: **consistent restraint**, **acknowledged revision**, and **scope partition**.
- **Step 7.a:** the substitution test applies to the claim as announced by the strong pole, not to a charitably weakened thesis.
- **Step 7.b:** the retracted pole of an established modal retraction cannot be credited as a genuine concession.
- **ARGUS Light:** the new control is not included in the short protocol.

The control was validated on real texts, by blind cross-audit between two model architectures, and with a ten-text adversarial guard set designed around the acknowledged-revision boundary.

## Languages and normative source

This release contains the protocol in six languages: French, English, Spanish, German, Italian and Portuguese.

The **French source is normative**. The five other language files are audited translations of the same V5.1.0 protocol.
